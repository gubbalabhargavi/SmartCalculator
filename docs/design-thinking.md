# Design Thinking — Loan Eligibility Checker

## C1.1 — Empathize (3 questions)
1. What information do you currently gather before applying for a loan, and how confident do you feel about it?
2. Have you ever been confused or surprised by a loan rejection reason?
3. Would you rather get an instant estimate with less detail, or a slower, more accurate check?

## C1.2 — Define (How Might We)
How might we help Meridian Bank customers instantly understand their loan
eligibility and the reasons behind it, so they can make informed borrowing
decisions without visiting a branch?

## C1.3 — Ideate (two solution strategies)
1. **Rule-based deterministic engine**: apply fixed thresholds (e.g. debt-to-income ratio) to give an instant, fully explainable yes/no with reasons.
2. **Risk-scoring model**: use a broader data set (credit score, transaction history) to produce a more accurate probability-based result, paired with a simplified explanation layer for transparency.
